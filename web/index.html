Predictions, published before kickoff. Every result, shown after.

A Poisson model predicts each match before it happens. The prediction is written to a database that will not let it be edited or deleted, so the record here is exactly what was said in advance — wins and losses both. Every page on this site queries that database live.

Track record

One match on this record is graded wrong, and it will stay that way. On 2026-09-06, TheSportsDB marked Arsenal vs Chelsea "finished" with a score of 1-1 just 45 minutes after kickoff — long before a real final whistle. The match was graded on that score. The real final score, 2-1 to Arsenal, arrived in a later sync, but a graded result can never be edited or deleted, so the wrong grade is permanent. Full writeup in Known data incidents; look for the ⚠ badge on Results and Markets.
